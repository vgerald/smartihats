The Local After-Responder

The most underrated part of a smart home is who answers when it breaks. We’re a team that lives in the homes we service — when a call comes in, the answer is a person, not a queue in another time zone.

What this engagement looks like

📞 One number that answers

You tell us what’s happening. A human who’s trained on your specific build is on the other end. No “press 4” menu-maze.

🚗 On-site within the day

For the things that need a pair of hands in your home — a dead camera, a bad switch, a firmware update that broke a scene — we can usually be on site the same day.

🔁 Proactive fixes

We watch the stack, see the firmware updates that tend to misbehave in local hardware, and apply safe fixes before they land on you at 2 a.m.

🤝 Ownership stays yours

Support isn’t a lock. We hold your admin access for maintenance, but you always have the key. If you ever leave, you keep the system, and it keeps working.

What the “after” actually includes

Monitoring, diagnostics, the same-day on-site visit when needed, firmware and security patch watch, and one number that answers. The scope is yours to choose — from light monitoring to full coverage.

The 2 a.m. problem, solved

Smart homes often break at the worst possible times: a lock that won’t unlock, a garage door that’s stuck, a thermostat that resets to 72. When you don’t have a local on-call, you’re troubleshooting in the dark. That’s the gap we fill.

Pricing model

Residency is a flat, simple price that scales with the size of your install. We tell you exactly what’s included when we send you the proposal.

Good for…

Anyone who values a supported smart home over a well-configured one. If the thought of a 3 a.m. garage-door failure keeps you up, this is the fix.
