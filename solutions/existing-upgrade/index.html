Solution

Upgrade Your Existing Smart Home

You don’t need to throw things away. We audit what you own, rescue the pieces that are worth keeping, and build a clean, unified system around them.

What this engagement looks like

💳 No rip-and-replace

We start with what you already have. A working camera or lock stays in play when it makes sense — no wasteful full teardown.

🧱 Gap analysis

We map what’s missing: a smart hub, proper local storage, scenes that actually span rooms, backup power where it matters.

🔗 Unified control

Whatever survives the audit is commissioned into the same single app, so you stop toggling between old brands and new ones.

📄 Written handoff

You get a document: what we found, what we changed, what we left alone, and why. No mystery, no black box.

A two-part process

First we audit and scope. Then we execute — usually in one or two focused weeks, with a hard stop on your calendar.

What “adopted” means

Adoption means it’s documented, tested to spec, and folded into the single app. If a device can’t be safely adopted, we tell you that and why, with the replacement option priced in.

Your devices, still yours

Nothing we install locks you in. The gear you bought stays yours; the gear we add comes with a clear ownership model.

Good for…

Homes that have “smart” devices but no plan. If you have cameras in one corner and a thermostat app you barely remember, this is the fix.

Turn your home into the easy one

A free on-site consultation maps your space, your gear, and your goals into a fixed-scope plan.
