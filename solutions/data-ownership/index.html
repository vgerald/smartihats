Data Ownership & Privacy

Your smart home is not supposed to be a data center. We design systems where your recordings, routines, and AI models stay in your home — and we tell you, in plain English, exactly where everything lives.

What this engagement looks like

📼 Local storage

Cameras record to storage in your home. Playback stays available when the internet — or a cloud provider — is down.

🧠 Local AI (when it makes sense)

Face-recognition and anomaly detection are opt-in, per residence, and run on hardware you own. What it sees, it sees locally.

🔒 No cloud hostage situation

Nothing we install holds your data in a cloud vault that a provider can price up. Your recordings are yours, on your device, in your house.

📄 A clear data map

We hand you a one-page diagram: what runs where, what goes to a cloud, and why. No mystery, no “trust us.”

What we never do

We never ship your camera feeds to a data center for storage. We never require a cloud subscription for basic playback. We never sell or share your data. Ever.

What we do do, and why

Some features — like remote access when you’re outside your LAN — require a secure tunnel. We use them, but we build them in a way you can audit: the keys are yours, the paths are known, and the traffic is encrypted.

Privacy as a spec, not a vibe

We treat privacy the way we treat power: something you decide on day one, in the design. Not a checkbox on the privacy page of a camera you bought in 2023.

Good for…

Anyone who wants a smart home without renting their privacy. If you have cameras and a cloud subscription, let’s talk about what local storage would change.
