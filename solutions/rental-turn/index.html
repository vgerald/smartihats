Rental / Turnover Package

A well-run short-term rental is a smart home with a lease on it. We build for the turnover workflow in mind — lights, locks, and scenes that run on a schedule, and that you can observe and adjust from wherever you are.

What this engagement looks like

🔑 Guest-ready handoff

The tenant gets a home that runs itself: lights, thermostat, scenes, and a lock that works on day one. No “here’s the code sheet” scramble at check-in.

📡 Your oversight, preserved

You keep full visibility: locks, cameras, and the status of every room. When a guest leaves, your view of the home stays exactly where you want it.

🔁 Turnover automation

Scenes that run on schedule: lights on at dusk, locks secured, thermostat set to an energy-conscious value while the unit is unoccupied.

🛠️ Maintenance visibility

A dead camera or a leak sensor is a notification you can act on — from anywhere in the world — before it becomes a lost booking or a water bill.

The turnover problem, solved

Rental owners live in the gap between “guest experience” and “asset maintenance.” When a smart home is bolted on without a plan, the owner ends up juggling apps, keys, and tenants. When it’s designed for the workflow, the same devices that delight the guest also monitor the asset.

What’s in the package

Locks, cameras, local storage, scenes, and a monitoring workflow. Everything is documented so the owner (or a property manager) can hand it off to the next tenant without a re-orient.

Pricing model

Flat, by unit. You know what it costs to bring a unit up to standard, and what it costs to keep it there.

Good for…

Owners of rentals, short-term stays, and staffed properties. If you manage more than one unit, this is the multiplier.
