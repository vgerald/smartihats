Solution

Whole-Home New Build

New construction is the best time to do this right. We design the wiring, the power, and the placement before anything is purchased — so the smart home is part of the architecture, not a retrofit.

What this engagement looks like

📐 Pre-frame design

Before drywall, we decide where cameras, sensors, and access points go. Every device has network and power — not a power strip on the floor.

🔌 Right-sized network

Wi-Fi, Ethernet backbone, and local storage sized for the number of devices you’ll actually have in two years.

🎬 Scenes and routines first

We design the day you want to live in before we design the gear that enables it. The room tells us the device — not the other way round.

🔑 Day-one handoff

At walk-through, the whole home works from one app, and you’re shown every routine. Not a demo — a test drive.

What we deliver at build-out

A smart-home schedule that runs alongside the general contractor: low-voltage wiring, camera placement, network backbone, and local storage — all coordinated so nothing gets painted over or buried.

Why pre-frame is not optional

Retrofits force compromises: extra conduit runs, underpowered circuits, cameras in the wrong spot. A pre-frame smart home gets the right wiring and the right placement, with no trade-offs.

Pricing model

Because everything is scoped before install, you get a fixed-scope price. We know the square footage, the device count, and the labor — so you get one number, not a range with asterisks.

Good for…

New builds and major remodels. If the builder hasn’t started low-voltage work yet, get us in the room now.

A free on-site consultation maps your space, your gear, and your goals into a fixed-scope plan.
