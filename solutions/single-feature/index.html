Single-Feature Add-Ons

Sometimes the ask is specific: “I want a face-recognition doorbell.” or “Just light the front walk better.” We do exactly that — a focused, documented install that fits in a day or two.

What this engagement looks like

🎯 Focused scope

One thing, done well and documented. No upselling, no scope creep, no “while we’re in there…” surprises on the invoice.

🔌 Clean install

Proper power, proper network, proper documentation. Even a single device deserves a clean job — the difference between a toy and an asset.

🧩 Ready to join the bigger picture

When you want to expand, the add-on is already commissioned into the platform. Adding the second room on a Friday is a 2-hour job, not a new project.

📄 Documented

You get the same written handoff as a whole-home build: what we installed, where it lives, how to reach it.

Examples of single-feature installs

Face-recognition doorbell · a smart lighting zone in the front entry · a smart lock on the back door · a single camera on the garage · a smart thermostat swap · an occupancy sensor in one room.

Why this beats a DIY kit

A DIY kit gets you to working. SmartiHats gets you to supported. The device is placed right, powered right, commissioned right, and someone local knows it by name.

Turnaround

Most single-feature installs are scoped in a 20-minute call and installed within the week. We keep a small buffer for supply and weather, but the install day itself is one or two.

Good for…

A specific improvement you’ve wanted for a while but don’t want a whole renovation to get.
