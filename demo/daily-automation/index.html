Daily Automation

Here’s what a typical day looks like with the routines we build. Press Play the day and watch the timeline advance.

The point of the demo is the seamlessness. None of these moments require you to open an app. The house handles itself on your schedule, and you only step in when you want to.

Every one of these moments is a routine we can configure to your real life: your commute times, your kids’ bedtime, your thermostat preferences, your cameras’ armed windows.

A full day, hour by hour

The exact scene an install at 8 p.m. would trigger — play the day and watch the automation fire.

6:30 AM

Good Morning

Lights warm up gradually. Thermostat to 68. Doors remain locked until you unlock.

8:15 AM

Commute

Front door locks. Cameras arm. Thermostat drops to 65 for the day.

12:00 PM

Midday

Occupancy sensors report empty. No motion. All clear.

5:45 PM

Coming Home

Front lights warm up. Thermostat climbs to 70. Porch light on.

8:00 PM

Dinner

Kitchen lights to 80%. Side lamps on. Music low on the kitchen speaker.

11:30 PM

Good Night

All non-security lights off. Doors lock. Cameras arm. Thermostat to 66.
