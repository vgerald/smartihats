Smart Security Walkthrough

Follow one night at a SmartiHats home, from “lock up” to “no one’s there.” This is a timeline simulation of the same event flow we install.

Here’s what a typical night actually looks like with our stack:

11:02 PM — You tap Away from the app. Doors lock, non-security lights off, cameras armed.

2:47 AM — Motion at the front walk. The local model identifies a stranger. You get a push alert with a short clip — no cloud round-trip, no “please log in to view your video.”

By 6:00 AM — You open the clip, see it’s a neighborhood cat, archive it. The recording is on your local drive, ready when you need it.
