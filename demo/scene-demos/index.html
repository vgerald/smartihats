Scene Demos

Click a scene and watch the house respond. This is a lightweight preview of the same scene engine we install at your home.

Scenes are the whole point of a unified smart home. Here are three we build for most homes:

Movie Night — lights dim to ~10%, side lamps go off, thermostat holds, doors lock.

Away — all non-security lights off, locks secured, cameras armed, thermostat to energy mode.

Good Morning — lights warm up gradually, thermostat to comfort, side lamps on, doors still locked until you unlock.
