Service area

Sandy Springs & Metro Atlanta. Here’s the coverage map and what’s on-site and what’s remote.

📍 On-site coverage

Sandy Springs, Buckhead, Dunwoody, Roswell, Alpharetta, Peachtree Corners, Marietta, and surrounding Metro Atlanta. We live here — same-day on-site service is the default on the residency plan.

🌐 Remote coverage

Anything inside the U.S. where a SmartiHats system is already installed. Remote diagnostics, monitoring, and on-call support are available anywhere with an internet connection.

Why coverage matters more than you think

A smart-home team that’s a flight away can fix your firmware, but they can’t reach your wiring. When the lock is stuck or the camera is dead, the only fix that works is someone on site — and that someone has to live near you. That’s the whole point of being local.
