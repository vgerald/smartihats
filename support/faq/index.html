Support

Frequently asked questions

The things every new homeowner asks before we start. If yours isn’t here, call us.

What does the install actually take?

Most add-ons are a day or two. Whole-home installs are scoped to your square footage and device count, typically two to three weeks with a hard stop on your calendar. We send the timeline before we start.

Who keeps the admin key?

You do. Day one. We hold it for maintenance while you’re on the residency plan, but you always have access and you can revoke ours anytime.

What happens when the internet is down?

Local cameras keep recording, local AI keeps running, and scenes that don’t need the cloud keep working. Remote access pauses — that part requires the tunnel — but the home still runs for the people in it.

Do I have to use your brand of cameras, locks, and thermostats?

No. We work with the major brands you’re already comfortable with. If you have gear you like, it stays in the design. If we recommend a different model, we’ll tell you why — and price the difference.

What about cloud subscriptions?

We build local-first for the parts that matter: local recording, local AI, local control. The only cloud touch we use is a secure tunnel for remote access — and we tell you, in plain English, exactly what goes where.

Can you work with my existing smart home?

Yes. That’s what the Add-On and Existing-Upgrade plans are for. We audit, adopt, fix, and unify — we don’t rip and replace what’s working.

How long does the support relationship last?

Indefinitely. The residency plan is month-to-month, and you hold the admin key from day one. If you ever leave, the system keeps working — you own it.

What do we do about firmware updates that break things?

We watch the firmware stream for the devices in your install. When a major update lands, we test it on a staging rig before we push it to your home — and we roll back the moment we see a regression.

Still have a question?

Call or email — we answer.
