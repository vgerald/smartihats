Feature

Cameras you own. Recordings you keep.

24/7 recording to storage in your home, with AI event detection — and without a monthly cloud vault you didn’t sign up for.

📼 24/7 local recording

Every camera writes to storage you control. Playback stays available even when the internet — or the provider — is down.

🔔 Smarter alerts

Get notified for the events that matter — a person at the door, a car in the drive — not a push for every leaf that blows across the frame.

🔌 Power-loss resilience

On a network outage, local cameras keep recording and stay viewable on your LAN. Your security system should not depend on the cloud staying up.

🛠️ No export maze

Need the footage? It’s on your device, in your home. No “export your video” workflow that costs extra or takes days.

The hidden cost of cloud cameras

Most “smart” cameras trade convenience for captivity: the best playback window is behind a subscription, the full history is in someone else’s cloud, and the price is allowed to change any time. When privacy or pricing becomes a concern, you have very little to hold.

SmartiHats sizes local storage for the days and cameras you actually want, and connects detection to it. The result is a camera system that remains yours — in every sense — for the life of the home.

Sized to fit

We size storage and retention to your budget and your comfort level — from a short rolling window to a multi-week archive.
