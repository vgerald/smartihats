Feature

One app. Every device. Zero juggling.

Your whole home — lights, locks, climate, cameras, garage, audio, and scenes — controlled from a single app. One login, one place to look, one number to call.

🎚️ Unified control

Flip the porch light, lock the door, and drop the temp to 70 from one screen — no app switching, no “is this the right login?”

🔁 Scenes that just work

One tap — Movie Night, Away, Good Night — orchestrates lights, locks, and heat together. Scenes are the whole point.

📱 Voice & automation included

Connect your favorite voice assistant. Automations like “when the door locks at night, run Good Night” are built, not bolted on.

🧩 Mixed brands, one brain

Zigbee, Z-Wave, WiFi, Matter — we speak every language your gear does and translate it into one clean interface.

What this fixes for you

Modern homes quietly accumulate a drawer of apps. Each one is a credential, a cloud dependency, and a support queue. When the power drops and everything half-restarts, you need eight apps to piece your home back together.

SmartiHats consolidates all of it into one platform you own. Every device we install is commissioned into that single app, tested end-to-end, and documented. If we ever add a capability, it lands inside the same app — not a new one on your home screen.

The day-one test

Before we hand you the keys, we prove it: you run a full home tour from the one app, start to finish, while we watch. No “that should work.”
